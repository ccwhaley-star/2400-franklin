    Who it's for

    One bay, four ways.

    Same shop floor, same private restroom, same roll-up door in back and storefront on Franklin Road. What goes on the floor is up to you.

    • Makers and fabricators

      Benches and tools along the wall, a desk by the window, parts and prototypes on the shelving, and the roll-up door for what comes in on a pallet.

    • Trades and service crews

      Back the truck in through the roll-up door, stage the next job, keep ladders, tools and stock dry and locked, and run paperwork from the counter.

    • Showrooms and small brands

      Samples on the shelves, a table to meet clients at, a storefront door on Franklin Road for walk-ins, and inventory next door at Free State Storage.

    • Motorsports and small shops

      A lift on the floor, tool chests and a compressor along the wall, a desk by the window, and the roll-up door so bikes roll straight in.

    Questions

    What people ask first.

    • What is a flex bay?

      Most bays are 900 SF (30 × 30 ft); the front-corner Bay H is 540 SF (18 × 30 ft). Each has a full-height shop floor and a private restroom. Each bay has a roll-up door at the back and a storefront entry on Franklin Road. Bay A, the flex suite, adds a steel mezzanine office above the front end.

    • Can I lease more than one bay?

      Yes. Bays combine side by side, so you can start with one bay and add the next one when you need it without moving. Two bays make 1,800 SF; three make 2,700 SF.

    • Where is 2400 Franklin?

      On Franklin Road in east Lawrence, across from VenturePark and next to East Hills Business Park, with K-10 right there: about 30 minutes to Olathe. Free State Storage, our sister property, is next door.

    • Can I park a truck or trailer and store inventory?

      Yes. Free State Storage, our sister property next door, has space for overflow, inventory, and truck, trailer and equipment parking.

    • How do I see a bay?

      Call (785) 727-2112, email leasing@2400franklin.com or send the form on this page with what you plan to use the space for. We reply within one business day and set up a tour.
