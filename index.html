Questions

What people ask first.

  • What is a flex bay?

    A 900 SF unit with a full-height shop floor, a steel mezzanine office above it, a kitchenette and a private restroom. Each bay has a roll-up door at the back and a storefront entry on Franklin Road.

  • Can I lease more than one bay?

    Yes. Bays combine side by side, so you can start with one 900 SF bay and add the next one when you need it without moving. Two bays make 1,800 SF; three make 2,700 SF.

  • What does a bay cost?

    Base rent starts at $950 a month. The NNN estimate is about $225 a month for 2027. Leases run three years with 3% annual steps.

  • Can I park a truck or trailer and store inventory?

    Yes. Free State Storage, our sister property next door, has space for overflow, inventory, and truck, trailer and equipment parking.

  • How do I see a bay?

    Email leasing@2400franklin.com or send the form on this page with what you plan to use the space for. We reply within one business day and set up a tour.
